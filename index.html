Shorts Studio

Shorts Studio is a personal, self-hosted tool used by a single creator to prepare short educational videos (true stories told with original animated drawings) and to send the finished video to the creator's own TikTok account as a draft.

The creator reviews every draft inside the TikTok app (caption, hashtags, AI-generated content label, visibility) and decides whether to publish it. Nothing is ever posted automatically.

  • Only the creator's own account is connected.
  • Permissions used: user.info.basic (show which account is connected), video.upload (send a draft to the TikTok inbox), video.list and user.info.stats (show the creator their own videos' and account's statistics, to see which videos work best).
  • No TikTok data is sold, shared or used for any other purpose.
