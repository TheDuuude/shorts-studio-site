Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

What is collected

When the Owner connects one of their own TikTok accounts, the tool receives from TikTok, for that account: an access token and a refresh token, the account's open ID, display name and avatar. With the creator's permission, the tool also reads the statistics of the Owner's own accounts and own videos (follower, like and video counts; views, likes, comments and shares of each video), only to show the Owner which videos perform best. No messages, no follower lists and no data about other people are requested or collected. No data is collected about any other person.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored encrypted on the Owner's own computer at home. They are never sent to any third party except TikTok itself, when uploading a draft.

What it is used for

Only to show which account is connected, to show the Owner their own statistics, and to send the Owner's validated videos to their TikTok inbox as drafts. Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when the Owner clicks "Disconnect" in the tool, and can also be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).
